What the tutorial covers

1

Why external control is limited. Fixed reasoning scripts are brittle across tasks, and search-heavy methods pay in tokens and latency — both blind to the model's evolving internal state.

2

The endogenous state. A compact four-number, dynamics-geometric state that decides which past evidence to keep active and when to stop — closing reasoning into a self-driven loop.

3

That it works and generalizes. Higher accuracy with 65.5% fewer tokens and 47.6% lower latency, across 3 LLMs and 2 VLM scales (16 datasets), plus training-free, embedding-only, and black-box-judge variants.
